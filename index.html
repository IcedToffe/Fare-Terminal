Bakit ito ang rates?

Default values: Aircon bus ₱15 for the first 5 km + ₱2.65 per succeeding km; Ordinary bus ₱13 for the first 5 km + ₱2.25 per succeeding km (the LTFRB bus matrix before the March 2026 adjustment). The March 2026 adjustment (aircon ₱18 + ₱2.98, ordinary ₱15 + ₱2.49) and the separate provincial bus matrix may apply to your bus. Check the matrix posted inside the bus and update these numbers if it differs.
